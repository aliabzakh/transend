# Transend logo: Held

The mark is one figure with raised arms around a gold coin: a person celebrating, or hands holding the money. It replaces the paper-cut family mark from the Draft A design drafts.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `transend-mark.svg` | Default. Leaf arms, Gold coin, on the Night ground (the app) |
| `transend-mark-forest.svg` | Forest arms, Gold coin, on Cream or white (print, deck) |
| `transend-mark-mono.svg` | One colour, black. The printed card and anything stamped or embroidered |
| `transend-app-icon.svg` | App icon and favicon: the mark on a Night rounded square |

**Geometry.** 96 × 96 grid. Coin: circle at (48, 27), radius 15. Arms: one quadratic curve from (16, 44) to (80, 44) with its control point at (48, 104), 14 wide, round caps. Only these two shapes, so it stays clear down to 16 px.

**Colours.** Leaf `#2FCB7A`, Gold `#E0A526`, Forest `#0F5132`, Night `#0B1A13`. Flat fills only, with no gradients or shadows.

**Wordmark.** "Transend" in Satoshi 700, letter-spacing −0.02em, in `--color-fg` on Night or Forest on Cream. Set the mark about 1.3 × the wordmark's cap height, with a gap of about a third of the mark's width. In the app header the mark is 24 px.
